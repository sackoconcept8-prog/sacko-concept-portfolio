A FEW ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Another question? Message me on WhatsApp.

How do I order a package?

Click Order on your chosen package to open the project brief directly. Include your package name and requirements. The studio will contact you to confirm scope, timing and payment.

Can I pay with PayPal?

You can request a PayPal invoice from the studio. The payment link is sent when your project is confirmed. Always use the official invoice or payment link for your order.

When does the delivery timeline start?

After brief approval, receipt of the required materials and payment confirmation. The exact schedule is agreed before work begins; displayed timelines are estimates.

Which files will I receive?

File formats and deliverables are listed in your package and confirmed in your quote. If you need editable source files, Canva or Figma formats, include this in your brief.

Are the templates editable and reusable?

Graphic kits are made to order. We agree on the design tool, template count, source files and usage rights together. Resale or redistribution requires explicit agreement.

Are the portfolio projects client commissions?

This selection presents independent concepts and design studies created by Sacko Concept. Demonstration brands illustrate a creative direction and are not client endorsements.
