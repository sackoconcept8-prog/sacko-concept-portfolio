What does each pack contain?

Each listing includes 33 Word templates, a company profile, 3 Excel systems in blank and demo editions (6 files), 6 PDFs and a video walkthrough. The systems cover HR document control, recruitment and onboarding, and performance and development.

Can I edit the files?

The Word templates and Excel workbooks are customisable. The PDFs are supporting resources. Work on copies and read the instructions before changing formulas.

What language are the documents in?

The French edition contains documents in French; the English edition contains documents in English. The two products are sold separately. Check the edition name on Chariow before paying.

What software do I need?

Microsoft Word and Excel desktop applications are recommended, along with a PDF reader. Software licences are not included. Full compatibility with other applications is not stated.

How do I access the files after purchase?

These are digital downloads. Follow the instructions and delivery arrangements displayed by Chariow when you purchase. No physical product is shipped.

Can I use the documents for my business?

Yes, within the organisation covered by your licence. You may keep, customise and use the files for that internal purpose. Adapt sensitive documents and have them reviewed for your situation and country.

Can I share or resell the files?

Resale and unauthorised redistribution of source templates are prohibited. The licence covers one organisation. Read the included licence for the precise rights and contact us about use across multiple organisations.

How do I contact support?

Email contact@sackoconcept.com with your edition and order reference. The listings include technical support for 30 days after purchase.

Can I get a refund?

No specific money-back guarantee is confirmed on the listings reviewed. Check the terms shown when ordering and contact SACKO CONCEPT with any request, without prejudice to your statutory rights.

How do I receive the two bonuses?

Both bonuses are in French, regardless of the edition purchased. After purchasing on Chariow, email your order reference to contact@sackoconcept.com. Support will provide the 40 HR explanation-request templates and the French AI office guide after verifying your purchase. Delivery is manual, on request.

Can you customise the tools for my company?

Yes. We can review customisation of your branding, documents and trackers. This service is separate from the pack purchase and requires a quote. Contact us on WhatsApp to tell us what you need.
