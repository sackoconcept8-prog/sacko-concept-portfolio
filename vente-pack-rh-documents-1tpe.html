Essential information

Formats, usage and support.

Software and requirements

Desktop Microsoft Excel and Word are recommended for the XLSX and DOCX files. You also need a PDF reader and an MP4 player. Microsoft Office software is not included.

Opening, editing and printing tests in desktop Microsoft Excel were not performed during preparation of the pack. Compatibility with your edition must be checked; compatibility with every software version or spreadsheet application is not guaranteed.

Usage licence

One purchase covers one identified organisation or legal entity. It may customise the files, keep private working copies and use completed documents for its internal operations. There is no fixed expiry date for use of the purchased version.

Resale, sublicensing and public redistribution of source files are not permitted. Source files may not be supplied to an organisation outside the licence. Use for additional clients or entities requires separate licences or written permission.

Before using the pack

  • All files are in English; a French edition is not included.
  • Each input register has 500 prepared rows. Expanding this capacity requires updating and testing the dependent ranges.
  • No macros, native slicers, cross-file synchronisation, automated reminders, external integrations or automatic logo insertion are included.
  • Seven sensitive templates are grouped for local review. The Employment Agreement Outline is a preparation brief, not a ready-to-sign contract.
  • Recruitment, disciplinary and termination documents must be adapted to the relevant country and reviewed by qualified people before use.

Your questions

Before you order.

What language are the files in?

All Word files, Excel workbooks, guides, captions and instructions in this edition are in English.

How many Excel systems are included?

Three distinct systems. Each is supplied in a blank edition and a demonstration edition, giving you six Excel files in total.

Can I add my logo?

Yes, manually. Enter your company name in Company Setup in each workbook. Insert your logo using the software commands; changes do not automatically synchronise between files.

Can I use these documents in any country?

They provide a starting point for adaptation. Universal legal compliance is not promised. Sensitive templates require qualified review for the relevant country and circumstances.

Can I reuse the pack for all my clients?

The licence covers one organisation. Using the source collection for other clients or entities requires separate licences or written permission.

What does support cover?

Support covers download difficulties, clarification of the instructions and reproducible defects in the original files. Bespoke services and individual HR decisions are excluded.

Which terms apply to purchases on 1TPE?

Payment, invoicing and refund requests are governed by the 1TPE terms of sale, which include a contractual 30-day money-back guarantee. Rights to use the pack remain subject to its licence.
