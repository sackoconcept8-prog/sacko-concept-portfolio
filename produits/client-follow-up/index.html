Know who to contact next.

A simple Excel client and lead tracker for freelancers, service providers and small businesses. Keep contact details, sales stages, notes and next actions together.

Included

  • 500 contact rows with unique IDs.
  • Sales-stage dropdowns and next follow-up dates.
  • Automatic Due, Overdue, Upcoming and Closed statuses.
  • Summary counts, filters and a built-in quick-start guide.

Your download

One editable .xlsx workbook with Clients and Guide sheets. The template is in English and contains no macros.

How to use it

  1. Set the review date.
  2. Add each contact, stage, follow-up date and next action in the yellow cells.
  3. Filter the follow-up status to plan your day.
  4. Update the record after each interaction.

This workbook organizes your follow-ups. It does not send emails, schedule calendar reminders or sync with a CRM. Prepared capacity: 500 contacts. A spreadsheet application supporting .xlsx is required.

For use within your own business. Do not resell or redistribute the template. Digital download only.
