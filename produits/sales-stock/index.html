Inside the product

Know exactly what you get.

Keep sales and stock in one clear workbook.

A practical Excel template for small shops, resellers and product-based businesses. Register your products, record sales and stock movements, and see your current quantities at a glance.

Included

  • 100 product slots with opening stock and reorder levels.
  • 500 transaction rows for stock received, sales and other stock removed.
  • Automatic current-stock, sales-revenue and reorder calculations.
  • Dropdowns, entry checks, filters and a quick-start guide.

Your download

One editable .xlsx workbook with four sheets: Overview, Products, Movements and Guide. The template is in English and contains no macros.

How to use it

  1. Open the workbook in a spreadsheet application that supports .xlsx files.
  2. Register products and opening stock.
  3. Enter your movements in the yellow cells and review the calculated results.

Use one currency and one stock location per workbook. Prepared capacity: 100 products and 500 movements. Revenue is quantity multiplied by sale price; taxes, refunds, fees and business expenses are not calculated. This is an operational tracker, not a full accounting system.

For use within your own business. Do not resell or redistribute the template. Digital download only.
