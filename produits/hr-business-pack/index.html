HR & Business Documents Pack — 33 Editable Word Templates + 6 Excel Files | English Edition

Get the product ↗

Spend less time creating documents and more time running your business.

Bring structure to your everyday HR and administrative work with the SACKO CONCEPT HR & Business Documents Pack—a practical collection of editable templates, Excel trackers and visual dashboards.

Designed for entrepreneurs, small businesses, HR coordinators and administrative professionals, this toolkit helps you organize recruitment, employee onboarding, document control and team development.

📊 Three Excel systems. Six ready-to-customize files.

Each system includes a blank workbook for your own records and a demonstration workbook with fictional examples:

• HR Document Control: organize employee documents, monitor expiry and review dates, track policy acknowledgements and manage administrative tasks.

• Recruitment & Onboarding: follow candidates, schedule interviews, record evaluations and offers, and coordinate onboarding activities.

• Employee Performance & Development: track reviews, goals, individual KPIs, training, skills and development actions.

Each input register includes 500 prepared rows, with dropdown lists, data checks, formulas and linked Excel charts.

📝 33 editable Word templates

Start with structured templates for job descriptions, recruitment advertisements, interview evaluations, welcome letters, onboarding checklists, leave requests, equipment handovers, performance reviews, meeting minutes and employee departures.

The collection also includes nine detailed policies and procedures, covering conduct, IT use, remote work, information security, employee data, expenses, grievances, discipline and recruitment.

The 24 shorter templates include targeted completion instructions and fictional filled examples to guide customization.

📚 Supporting resources included

• A company profile document for branding and configuration.

• Six PDFs: user guide, document catalog, product preview, customization guide, license and troubleshooting FAQ.

• A captioned video walkthrough.

• Technical support for 30 calendar days after purchase.

Add your branding, adapt the templates and build a more organized way of working.

Digital download only. No physical product. Your purchase allows you to keep, customize and use the purchased files for one licensed organization, including working and backup copies. Completed documents may be delivered to their intended recipients. Resale and unauthorized redistribution of the source templates are prohibited.

Microsoft Excel and Word desktop applications are recommended. Sensitive employment documents require customization and qualified local review before use. The Employment Agreement Outline is a preparation brief, not a ready-to-sign contract.
