Orders & payments

Prices are displayed in US dollars (USD). Clicking Order opens a project brief and does not confirm the final project scope. The studio agrees deliverables, timing, formats and pricing with you before work begins.

Payments are processed by the provider shown on your invoice or official order link. This site does not collect card numbers or PayPal passwords. Returning to this site does not verify a payment.

Deliverables & use

Source files, formats and usage rights are defined in your project confirmation. Graphic template kits are made to order. Resale or redistribution requires explicit agreement. For cancellation, revision or refund enquiries, contact the studio with your project reference.

Forms & personal information

Brief links open forms hosted on Jotform or the Sacko Brief Studio website. Information you choose to send is used to understand your needs, prepare a quote and manage your project. Do not send passwords, card details or documents unrelated to your request.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of information sent to the studio, email contact@sackoconcept.com with your request. External platforms also apply their own privacy policies.
