Do I need technical skills?

None. Answer a few simple questions about your business and Ilano sets up your workspace for you. If you can send an email, you can use Ilano.

Can the workspace really use my logo and colors?

Yes. Your workspace takes your company name, your logo and your brand color automatically. Your team sees your brand, not ours.

Can my team use it on their phones?

Yes. Ilano works on phones, tablets and computers. Each team member gets their own access and only sees what you allow.

What happens after the free trial?

Your 14-day free trial starts the moment you sign up. Then choose the plan that fits your team — pay monthly, or save 10% on 3 months and 20% on 12 months. Renewals are automatic.
